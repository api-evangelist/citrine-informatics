- [Blog: Models Are Useless Unless You Use Them](https://citrine.io/models-are-useless-unless-you-use-them/): Models, in isolation, generate no value\. A model is an incredibly powerful tool—it combines expert knowledge with real\-world data to extract insight, uncover hidden relationships, and communicate what truly matters across teams\. But a powerful tool left idle on a desktop never helps anyone\. In materials, chemistry, and product development, a mindset shift is required: away from perfecting models in isolation and toward using data\-driven tools to design, test, and validate real materials that deliver real\-world value\.
